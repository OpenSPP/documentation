# Module installation

OpenSPP is built from individual modules that can be installed separately or as a bundle. A **product** — such as SP-MIS, Social Registry, or DRIMS — is a pre-configured bundle of modules designed to match a common social protection use case. Installing a product gives you everything you need for that use case in one step. If your needs don't fit a standard product, you can also install individual modules and combine them freely.
