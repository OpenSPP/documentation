# Key features

**Registration and data collection –** Build and maintain a continuously updated database through imports, system integrations, or direct registration. Structured workflows support reliable updates, whether initiated by citizens or registrars.

*Read more: {doc}`Unified and hierarchical beneficiary registry <../features/unified_registry>`*

---

**Targeting and eligibility –** With a flexible approach to eligibility and targeting, the OpenSPP SP-MIS can serve as a central data repository to support a wide array of programs.

*Read more: {doc}`Eligibility and targeting <../features/eligibility_targeting>`*

---

**Program and entitlement management –** Support the delivery of both cash and in-kind entitlements. Automate cash transfers via financial service providers, issue vouchers, or manage food and non-food item distribution, including stock and warehouse management.

*Read more: {doc}`End-to-end program and entitlement management <../features/program_management>`*

---

**Payment and disbursement –** Connect to multiple payment channels including banks, mobile money operators, and cash distribution points to disburse benefits efficiently and track payment status.

*Read more: {doc}`Pluggable payment and disbursement <../features/payment_disbursement>`*

---

**In-kind benefits and inventory –** Manage the full lifecycle of in-kind distributions, from procurement to warehouse stock and delivery, with real-time inventory tracking.

*Read more: {doc}`In-kind benefits and inventory management <../features/in_kind_benefits>`*

---

**Change management –** Route sensitive updates through configurable approval workflows with role-based controls and full audit trails. Track what changed, who made the change, and why, ensuring data integrity and accountability.

*Read more: {doc}`Auditable change management <../features/change_management>`*

---

**Grievance redress –** Provide a structured mechanism for beneficiaries and staff to raise, track, and resolve complaints, with SLA management and escalation rules.

*Read more: {doc}`Grievance Redress Mechanism (GRM) <../features/grievance_redress>`*

---

**Data integration and APIs –** Connect securely with other national databases (e.g., ID, health, civil registration) through RESTful APIs to pull information directly, ensuring data is up-to-date and interoperable.

*Read more: {doc}`Data integration and interoperability (APIs) <../features/data_integration_apis>`*
