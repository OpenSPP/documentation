# Try our products

Explore OpenSPP products using pre-built demo data. Each guide walks you through loading sample data and navigating key workflows so you can evaluate the product before implementing it.

**For:** Evaluators, new users, and trainers

## Available demo guides

**{doc}`Try OpenSPP SP-MIS <try_spmis/index>`**
Load the SP-MIS demo dataset and explore 6 social protection programs, 7 demo personas, and ~500 enrollments. Ideal for evaluating cash transfer and social assistance workflows.

**{doc}`Try OpenSPP Farmer Registry <try_farmer_registry/index>`**
Load the Farmer Registry demo dataset and explore 5 agricultural programs, 8 farm stories, cooperatives, and a full change request lifecycle. Ideal for evaluating agricultural input subsidy, livestock, and climate resilience workflows.

**{doc}`Try OpenSPP Disability Registry <try_disability_registry/index>`**
Load the Disability Registry demo data and explore WG-SS assessments, impairment classification, assistive device management, and CEL-based disability targeting. Ideal for evaluating inclusive social protection workflows.
